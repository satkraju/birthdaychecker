Privacy Policy

BirthdayChecker.com — Your privacy is important to us.

Last updated: May 2026 · Effective immediately upon publication.

1. Overview

BirthdayChecker.com ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at https://birthdaychecker.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Our birthday calculator runs entirely in your browser — the birth date you enter is never transmitted to our servers, never stored, and never shared with third parties.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

The following data is processed only locally in your browser and is never sent to us:

  • Your date of birth
  • Your age or any calculated birthday results
  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Any information you enter into the calculator

3. Information We Do Collect

Like virtually all websites, we collect limited anonymous technical information automatically when you visit:

  • Analytics data — page views, session duration, country/region (not city), browser type, device type, and referral source. This data is aggregated and anonymised — it cannot identify you personally.
  • Server logs — standard web server logs including IP address (anonymised after 24 hours), HTTP request type, and timestamp. Used solely for security monitoring and uptime.
  • Cookies — we use minimal functional cookies. See Section 5 below.

4. How We Use Information

The limited analytics data we collect is used only to:

  • Understand which features are most useful so we can improve the site
  • Monitor for security threats and abuse
  • Maintain site uptime and performance

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Analytics cookies — anonymous usage statistics (e.g. Google Analytics with IP anonymisation enabled, or a privacy-first alternative). You can opt out via your browser settings or browser extensions such as uBlock Origin.
  • No advertising cookies — we do not run behavioural advertising and set no ad-targeting cookies.

By continuing to use the site you consent to our use of essential and analytics cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

BirthdayChecker.com participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising programme designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

When you click a gift guide link and make a purchase on Amazon, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Amazon may set their own cookies when you click through. Please refer to Amazon's Privacy Notice for details on how they handle your data.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics (or equivalent) — anonymous traffic analytics. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon Associates — affiliate link programme. Amazon Privacy Notice.

We do not embed social media tracking pixels or share data with social networks.

8. Children's Privacy

BirthdayChecker.com is a general-audience website suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at support@birthdaychecker.com and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

If you are located in the European Union or California, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
  • Right to deletion — request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to opt out — opt out of any data sale (we do not sell data)
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate data

Because we collect only anonymised analytics data, there is typically no personal data to access or delete. To exercise any right, contact us at support@birthdaychecker.com.

10. Data Security

Our site is served over HTTPS (TLS encryption). Because we do not store personal data on our servers, the risk of a data breach exposing your personal information is minimal. Server logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days and then permanently deleted.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
